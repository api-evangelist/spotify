Our Spotify Sessions video series turns up the volume on some of Latin America’s most exciting artists, giving them and their hand-picked creative teams a space to reimagine their music. Over the last few weeks, we’ve dropped a trio of new sessions featuring artists from Argentina, Brazil, and Mexico. Spotify Sessions serves as a platform for both established and emerging names, bringing together an artist, a music director, and a visual director to create a five‑track video EP.
