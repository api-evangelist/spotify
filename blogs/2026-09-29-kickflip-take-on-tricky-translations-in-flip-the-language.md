An expression may be easy to use in one language, but nearly impossible to explain in another. That’s the challenge at the heart of Flip the Language, a new video podcast on Spotify. Across four weekly episodes, K-Pop boy group KickFlip explores Korean slang, memes, and everyday phrases that don’t quite translate word for word....
