Earlier this year, we introduced Reserved by Spotify, a new experience designed to give an artist’s most dedicated fans a better chance to see them live. Eligible Premium subscribers ages 18+ in the U.S. and Canada get a dedicated window to purchase up to two tour tickets held just for them before the general sale.
